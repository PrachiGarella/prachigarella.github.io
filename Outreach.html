The Mystery of Time: Is the Universe Stuck Moving Forward?

Patterson Park Patio Bar · Houston, Texas

I explored why everyday life has a direction in time: why a shattered glass does not put itself back together, and why we remember the past rather than the future. The discussion connected entropy and the second law of thermodynamics to the universe’s initial conditions, and considered what physics says about time travel.

  • The arrow of time
  • Entropy
  • The early universe

What Is the Universe Made of? The Quantum Field Story

The Library · Houston, Texas

I introduced the idea that fields fill space and particles are excitations of those fields. Through an intuitive discussion, we explored how this picture describes matter and forces, and how it changes the way we think about the universe’s fundamental ingredients.

Community outreach

Empowering Women in STEM

I advocate for women’s rights and representation in STEM. Through the Women in Physics Society, I help bring people together to discuss barriers, share experiences, and create opportunities for women in science.

Pint of Science

As part of the Houston organizing committee, I helped bring Pint of Science back to the United States after the pandemic. The festival brings researchers into pubs, cafés, and other public spaces to share their work and answer questions.

Pint of Science Houston ↗

Beyond Portals

I co-founded Beyond Portals to make fundamental science more accessible and support underrepresented communities in STEM. Through public talks, workshops, and seminars, we create spaces to learn, exchange ideas, and explore careers in science.
